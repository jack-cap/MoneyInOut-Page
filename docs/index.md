## Simple expense tracking. Free forever.

Track your money in and out without the complexity of traditional finance apps. No subscriptions, no ads, no account required.

## What It Does

Money In & Out helps you answer one simple question: **Where is my money going?**

- **Add transactions** - Manually or by scanning receipts
- **Organize with categories** - See spending patterns
- **View analysis** - Charts and breakdowns by day, week, month, or year
- **Sync across devices** - iPhone, iPad, and Mac with iCloud

That's it. No budgeting, no bank connections, no bill reminders. Just straightforward expense tracking.

## Key Features

- **AI Receipt Scanner** - Snap a photo, auto-fill details instantly
- **Quick Entry** - Add transactions in seconds
- **Smart Categories** - Color-coded organization
- **Visual Analysis** - Charts and spending breakdowns
- **iCloud Sync** - Seamless across all Apple devices
- **100% Private** - Your data never leaves your devices
- **Works Offline** - No internet required

## Is This App For You?

### ✅ Perfect if you want to:
- Track daily expenses simply
- Know where your money goes
- Scan receipts and auto-fill details
- Keep your financial data private
- Use across iPhone, iPad, and Mac

### ❌ Not for you if you need:
- Bank account connections
- Budgeting and forecasting tools
- Bill reminders and alerts
- Investment tracking
- Loan calculators
- Multi-user/family sharing

## What Makes It Different?

**Privacy First** - All AI processing happens on your device. Your receipts and transactions never touch our servers or any cloud AI service.

**Truly Free** - No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads. Free forever.

**Simple by Design** - We intentionally keep it simple. One thing done well beats ten things done poorly.
