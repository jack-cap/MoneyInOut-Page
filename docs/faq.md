# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Money In & Out.

## General

### Is this app right for me?

**You'll love Money In & Out if you:**
- Want simple expense tracking without complexity
- Value privacy and want data to stay on your devices
- Like scanning receipts to auto-fill details
- Use iPhone, iPad, or Mac (or all three)
- Want a free app with no ads or subscriptions

**Look elsewhere if you need:**
- Bank account connections
- Budgeting and forecasting tools
- Bill reminders and alerts
- Investment tracking
- Multi-user or family sharing

### Is Money In & Out really free?

Yes, completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads. Free forever.

### Why is it free?

We built Money In & Out because we wanted a simple expense tracker for ourselves. We're sharing it with others who want the same thing.

### Will you add a paid version?

No plans for that. The app will remain free.

### What platforms does it support?

- iPhone and iPad (iOS 26.0+)
- Mac (macOS 26.0+)

### Do I need an account?

No account required. Just download and use.

## Privacy & Data

### What data do you collect?

None. We don't collect any data about you or your transactions.

### Where is my data stored?

On your device and optionally in your iCloud account (if you enable sync).

### Can you see my transactions?

No. We have no access to your data. It's stored locally on your devices and in your private iCloud account.

### Is my data encrypted?

Yes. iCloud data is encrypted end-to-end. Local data is protected by your device security.

### Can I export my data?

Yes. Export to CSV format anytime from the app.

## Features

### Can I set budgets?

Not currently. Money In & Out focuses on tracking, not budgeting.

### Are there recurring transactions?

Not yet. You need to add recurring transactions manually each time.

### Can I attach receipts?

Yes! The receipt scanner automatically saves receipt images with transactions. You can also manually attach photos to any transaction.

### Is there a web version?

No. Money In & Out is Apple platforms only.

### Can I connect my bank?

No. Money In & Out doesn't connect to banks. Manual entry only.

### Can I share with family?

Not directly. Each person needs their own copy. Family sharing of the app is supported through App Store.

## Receipt Scanner

### How does the receipt scanner work?

Snap a photo → AI extracts merchant, amount, date → Review and save. Takes 2-3 seconds, works offline.  
**[Full receipt scanner guide →](user-guide/receipt-scanner.md)**

### Why is the app 1.02 GB?

We include a complete AI model (Qwen3 1.7B) inside the app for 100% privacy - your receipts never leave your device.

### Is my receipt data private?

Yes. All processing happens on your device. No cloud AI services, no uploads, works offline.

### How accurate is the scanner?

85-95% accurate. Always review auto-filled fields before saving - you can edit anything.

### What if I don't want to use the scanner?

Manual entry is always available and takes ~5 seconds.

## iCloud Sync

### Is iCloud sync required?

No, it's optional. iCloud sync is **ON by default** if you have iCloud Drive enabled, but you can turn it off in Settings if you prefer to keep data local only. The app works fine either way.

### Does sync cost money?

No. iCloud sync is free with your Apple ID. Money In & Out uses minimal storage and is automatically enabled if you have iCloud Drive turned on.

### How fast is sync?

Usually within seconds. Depends on your internet connection.

### What if I have sync issues?

Check that:
- iCloud Drive is enabled
- You're signed in with same Apple ID on all devices
- You have internet connection
- Money In & Out is enabled in iCloud settings

### Can I sync with Android?

No. iCloud sync only works with Apple devices.

## Usage

### How do I add a transaction?

Two ways:
1. **Scan Receipt**: Tap +, choose "Scan Receipt", take photo, review auto-filled details, save
2. **Manual Entry**: Tap +, choose "Manual Entry", enter details, save

### Can I edit transactions?

Yes. Tap any transaction to edit it.

### Can I delete transactions?

Yes. Swipe left on a transaction and tap Delete.

### How do I create categories?

Go to Categories section, tap Add Category, enter details, save.

### Can I change category colors?

Yes, when creating or editing a category.

### How do I see spending by category?

Go to the Analysis tab to see charts and breakdowns.

## Technical

### What iOS version do I need?

iOS 26.0+ for iPhone/iPad, macOS 26.0+ for Mac.

### How much storage does it use?

Very little. Typically less than 10 MB even with years of data.

### Does it work offline?

Yes. Add transactions offline, they'll sync when you're back online.

### Can I use on multiple devices?

Yes. Enable iCloud sync to use on all your Apple devices.

### What if I get a new device?

Install the app, sign in with your Apple ID, enable iCloud sync. Your data will download automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### App won't open

Try:
- Restart your device
- Check for iOS/macOS updates
- Reinstall the app

### Transactions not syncing

Check:
- iCloud Drive is enabled
- Same Apple ID on all devices
- Internet connection
- iCloud storage available

### Data disappeared

Check:
- Date range/period selection
- Filters applied
- iCloud sync status
- Try pull to refresh

### Can't add transaction

Ensure:
- Amount is valid number
- Category is selected
- App is up to date

## Data Management

### How do I backup my data?

iCloud sync acts as automatic backup. You can also export to CSV for additional backup.

### How do I restore data?

If using iCloud sync, just sign in on a new device. Otherwise, you'll need to import from a CSV export.

### Can I import data?

Not currently. Manual entry only.

### How do I delete all data?

Delete the app to remove local data. To remove iCloud data, disable sync and delete from iCloud settings.
