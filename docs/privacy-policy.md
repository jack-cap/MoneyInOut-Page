# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2025

## Overview

Money In & Out is committed to protecting your privacy. This app is designed with privacy as a core principle - no ads, no tracking, no data collection.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** All information you enter into Money In & Out remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third-party services.

### What We Don't Collect

- No personal information
- No transaction details
- No receipt images
- No usage analytics
- No crash reports
- No device information
- No location data
- Nothing. Zero. Nada.

## Receipt Scanner & On-Device AI

Money In & Out includes a receipt scanner powered by on-device AI:

### How It Works

- Receipt images are processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework
- Text extraction uses optical character recognition (OCR) running locally
- Smart parsing uses Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) when available
- All AI models run on your device - nothing is sent to the cloud
- Receipt images are stored locally with your transactions

### Your Privacy

- No receipt images are uploaded to any server
- No transaction data is sent externally
- All processing happens on-device
- Receipt images sync via your iCloud (if enabled) - never through our servers
- You can delete receipt images anytime

### On-Device AI Model

Money In & Out includes a complete AI language model (Qwen3 1.7B, 4-bit quantized):
- Bundled inside the app (~1 GB)
- Runs entirely on your device
- No data sent to Apple or any external service
- No internet connection required
- Completely private and secure

## Data Storage

### Local Storage

- All transaction and category data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData
- Receipt images are stored locally with transactions
- Data is stored in your device's secure storage area
- No data is accessible to other apps or services
- Protected by your device's security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)

### Your Control

- You own your data completely
- Delete any transaction anytime
- Delete any category anytime
- Uninstall the app to remove all local data

## iCloud Sync

### Optional Feature

iCloud sync is completely optional. You can use Money In & Out without ever enabling it.

### How iCloud Sync Works

If you choose to enable iCloud sync:

- Your data synchronizes across your Apple devices using Apple's CloudKit service
- Data is stored in your private iCloud account
- Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- We do not have access to your iCloud data
- Only you can access your data

### Apple's Privacy

iCloud sync is governed by Apple's privacy policy:

- [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- [iCloud Security Overview](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202303)

### Disabling iCloud Sync

You can disable iCloud sync anytime:

1. Go to device Settings
2. Tap your name > iCloud
3. Disable Money In & Out
4. Choose to keep or delete iCloud data
