# Installation

Get Money In & Out up and running in just a few minutes.

## System Requirements

### iOS
- **Version**: iOS 26.0 or later
- **Devices**: iPhone and iPad
- **Storage**: ~1 GB (includes AI model for receipt scanning)

### macOS
- **Version**: macOS 26.0 or later
- **Devices**: Apple Silicon or Intel Mac
- **Storage**: ~1 GB (includes AI model for receipt scanning)

### iCloud (Optional)
- iCloud account (your Apple ID)
- iCloud Drive enabled
- Used only if you want to sync across devices

## Download

### App Store
Download Money In & Out from the App Store:

[Download on the App Store](#){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

1. Open the App Store on your device
2. Search for "Money In & Out"
3. Tap "Get" to download
4. Open the app

## Why Is The App 1.02 GB?

The app includes a complete AI model (Qwen3 1.7B) for receipt scanning. This ensures 100% privacy - all processing happens on your device, no cloud services.

**[Learn about receipt scanning →](../user-guide/receipt-scanner.md)**

## First Launch

When you first open Money In & Out:

1. **Welcome Screen** - Brief introduction to the app
2. **iCloud Sync Prompt** - Choose whether to enable sync (optional)
3. **Ready to Use** - Start adding transactions!

Categories are created automatically as you add transactions - no setup needed.

## iCloud Sync

**iCloud sync is ON by default** if you have iCloud Drive enabled. Your data automatically syncs across all your devices.

### How It Works

- **Automatic** - Syncs in the background without any setup
- **Seamless** - Works across iPhone, iPad, and Mac
- **Private** - Data encrypted in your iCloud account
- **No action needed** - Just sign in with the same Apple ID on all devices

### Turn Off iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you prefer to keep data local only and NOT sync across devices:

#### On iPhone/iPad
1. Open **Settings** app
2. Tap **[Your Name]** at the top (Apple Account, iCloud and more)
3. Tap **iCloud**
4. Tap **Saved to iCloud** (or **Apps Using iCloud**)
5. Find **Money In & Out** and toggle **OFF**
6. Choose "Keep on My iPhone/iPad" or "Delete from My iPhone/iPad"

#### On Mac
1. Open **System Settings**
2. Click **[Your Name]** (Apple Account)
3. Click **iCloud**
4. Click **Saved to iCloud** (or **Apps Using iCloud**)
5. Find **Money In & Out** and toggle **OFF**
6. Choose "Keep on My Mac" or "Delete from My Mac"

!!! warning "Turning Off Sync"
    If you turn off iCloud sync, your data will only be stored locally on that device. Changes won't sync to your other devices.

## Install on Multiple Devices

To use Money In & Out on all your devices:

1. Download from App Store on each device
2. Sign in with the same Apple ID
3. Enable iCloud sync on all devices
4. Your data will sync automatically

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Download
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you have enough storage space
- Try restarting your device
- Check App Store for system requirements

### iCloud Sync Not Working
- Verify iCloud Drive is enabled
- Check you're signed in with the same Apple ID
- Ensure you have iCloud storage available
- Try toggling iCloud sync off and on

### App Won't Open
- Restart your device
- Check for iOS/macOS updates
- Reinstall the app if needed

## Uninstalling

If you need to remove Money In & Out:

### iPhone/iPad
1. Long press the app icon
2. Tap "Remove App"
3. Choose "Delete App"

### Mac
1. Open Finder
2. Go to Applications
3. Drag Money In & Out to Trash
4. Empty Trash

!!! warning "Data Deletion"
    Uninstalling the app will remove local data. If iCloud sync is enabled, your data remains in iCloud and will restore if you reinstall the app.
