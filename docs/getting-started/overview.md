# Overview

Welcome to Money In & Out - simple expense tracking that respects your privacy and time.

## What is Money In & Out?

Money In & Out is a straightforward expense tracker for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. It helps you track income and expenses without the complexity of traditional finance apps.

**Core Philosophy:**
- Simple - No complicated setup or learning curve
- Fast - Add transactions in seconds
- Private - Your data stays on your devices
- Free - No subscriptions, no ads, no hidden costs

## How It Works

### 1. Add Transactions

**Two ways to add:**

**Scan Receipt (Fastest)**
1. Tap the + button
2. Take a photo of your receipt
3. AI extracts merchant, amount, and date
4. Review and save

**Manual Entry**
1. Tap the + button
2. Enter amount and select category
3. Save

### 2. Smart Categories

The AI automatically organizes transactions into categories:
- **Auto-matching** - Recognizes merchants and assigns categories
- **Auto-creation** - Creates new categories when needed
- **Smart learning** - Remembers your preferences

Categories like Food & Dining, Transportation, Shopping, Entertainment, and more are created automatically based on your spending.

Each category has a color and icon for quick identification.

### 3. View Analysis

See your spending patterns:
- **By period** - Day, week, month, or year
- **By category** - Visual charts and breakdowns
- **Summary stats** - Total income, expenses, net balance

### 4. Sync Across Devices

Enable iCloud sync to keep data synchronized across:
- iPhone and iPad (iOS 26.0+)
- Mac (macOS 26.0+)

## What It Doesn't Do

Money In & Out intentionally keeps things simple:

- ❌ No bank connections
- ❌ No budgeting features
- ❌ No bill reminders
- ❌ No investment tracking
- ❌ No loan calculators
- ❌ No multi-user accounts

If you need these features, Money In & Out isn't the right fit.

## Platform Requirements

- **iOS**: 26.0+ (iPhone and iPad)
- **macOS**: 26.0+
- **iCloud**: Optional, for sync across devices
- **Storage**: ~1 GB (includes AI model for receipt scanning)

## Privacy & Security

- **Zero data collection** - We don't collect anything
- **On-device processing** - All AI runs locally
- **No third-party services** - No analytics, no tracking
- **Your data only** - Stored on your devices and your iCloud
- **No account required** - Download and use immediately
