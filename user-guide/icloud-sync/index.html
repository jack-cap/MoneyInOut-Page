    iCloud Sync¶

    Keep your financial data in sync across all your Apple devices with iCloud.

    What is iCloud Sync?¶

    iCloud sync is ON by default if you have iCloud Drive enabled on your device.

    Money In & Out automatically syncs your data across:

    • iPhone
    • iPad
    • Mac

    Changes on one device appear on all devices within seconds. No setup required - just sign in with the same Apple ID.

    How It Works¶

    Automatic Sync¶

    • Real-time - Changes sync immediately
    • Automatic - No manual sync needed
    • Seamless - Works in background
    • Reliable - Handles conflicts automatically

    What Syncs¶

    Everything syncs:

    • All transactions
    • Custom categories
    • Category colors and icons
    • Settings and preferences

    How iCloud Sync Works¶

    Automatic Sync¶

    iCloud sync is enabled by default if:

    • You're signed in with your Apple ID
    • iCloud Drive is enabled on your device
    • You have available iCloud storage

    No additional setup needed - it just works!

    Requirements¶

    • iCloud account (Apple ID)
    • iCloud Drive enabled
    • Internet connection (for syncing)
    • Sufficient iCloud storage

    Turning Off iCloud Sync¶

    If you want to keep data local only and NOT sync:

    On iPhone/iPad¶

    1. Open Settings
    2. Tap [Your Name] at top (Apple Account, iCloud and more)
    3. Tap iCloud
    4. Tap Saved to iCloud (or Apps Using iCloud)
    5. Find Money In & Out
    6. Toggle OFF
    7. Choose "Keep on My iPhone/iPad" or "Delete from My iPhone/iPad"

    On Mac¶

    1. Open System Settings
    2. Click [Your Name] (Apple Account)
    3. Click iCloud
    4. Click Saved to iCloud (or Apps Using iCloud)
    5. Find Money In & Out and toggle OFF
    6. Choose "Keep on My Mac" or "Delete from My Mac"

    First Sync¶

    When you first enable sync:

    1. App uploads existing data
    2. May take a few moments
    3. Watch for sync indicator
    4. Wait for completion

    Using Multiple Devices¶

    Initial Setup¶

    On each device:

    1. Download Money In & Out
    2. Sign in with same Apple ID
    3. Enable iCloud sync
    4. Wait for data to sync

    Adding Transactions¶

    Add on any device:

    • Transaction appears on all devices
    • Usually within seconds
    • Automatic and seamless

    Editing Transactions¶

    Edit on any device:

    • Changes sync to all devices
    • Most recent change wins
    • Conflicts resolved automatically

    Sync Status¶

    Indicators¶

    Look for sync status:

    • Syncing - Cloud icon with arrows
    • Synced - Checkmark or no indicator
    • Error - Warning icon

    Check Sync Status¶

    To verify sync:

    1. Add transaction on one device
    2. Check other device
    3. Should appear within seconds
    4. Pull to refresh if needed

    Conflict Resolution¶

    What are Conflicts?¶

    Conflicts occur when:

    • Same transaction edited on two devices
    • Both devices offline, then sync
    • Rare but possible

    How Conflicts are Resolved¶

    Money In & Out uses "last write wins":

    • Most recent change is kept
    • Older change is discarded
    • Automatic resolution
    • No user action needed

    Avoiding Conflicts¶

    Best practices:

    • Wait for sync before editing
    • Avoid editing same transaction on multiple devices
    • Ensure good internet connection

    Troubleshooting¶

    Sync Not Working¶

    If data isn't syncing:

    1. Check iCloud Drive
    2. Ensure enabled in Settings

    3. Verify Money In & Out is enabled

    4. Check Internet

    5. Need active connection

    6. Try different network

    7. Check iCloud Storage

    8. Ensure space available

    9. Money In & Out uses minimal space

    10. Restart App

    11. Close completely
    12. Reopen

    13. Wait for sync

    14. Restart Device

    15. Sometimes needed
    16. Fixes connection issues

    Duplicate Transactions¶

    If you see duplicates:

    • Usually temporary during sync
    • Wait a few moments
    • Should resolve automatically
    • If persists, contact support

    Missing Transactions¶

    If transactions missing:

    1. Check all devices are syncing
    2. Verify same Apple ID
    3. Check date range/filters
    4. Wait for sync to complete
    5. Try pull to refresh

    Sync Taking Too Long¶

    If sync is slow:

    • Check internet speed
    • Large data sets take longer
    • Be patient on first sync
    • Subsequent syncs are faster

    Privacy & Security¶

    Your Data is Private¶

    • Encrypted - End-to-end encryption
    • Your iCloud - Stored in your account
    • No third parties - Only you have access
    • Apple's security - Protected by Apple

    What Apple Sees¶

    Apple cannot see:

    • Your transaction details
    • Your amounts
    • Your categories
    • Your notes

    Apple only sees:

    • Encrypted data
    • Storage usage

    Disabling Sync¶

    To stop syncing:

    1. Go to iCloud settings
    2. Disable Money In & Out
    3. Choose to keep or delete data

    Disabling Sync

    Disabling sync doesn't delete data from devices, but they won't sync anymore.

    iCloud Storage¶

    How Much Space?¶

    Money In & Out uses minimal storage:

    • Typical: Less than 1 MB
    • Even with years of data
    • Text data is very small

    If Storage Full¶

    If iCloud storage is full:

    • Sync may stop working
    • Free up space or upgrade
    • Money In & Out needs very little

    Managing Storage¶

    To check usage:

    1. Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
    2. Tap Manage Account Storage (or Manage Storage)
    3. Find Money In & Out
    4. See usage details

    Backup & Restore¶

    Automatic Backup¶

    iCloud sync acts as backup:

    • Data always in cloud
    • Safe from device loss
    • Restore by signing in

    Restore Data¶

    To restore on new device:

    1. Install Money In & Out
    2. Sign in with Apple ID
    3. Enable iCloud sync
    4. Data downloads automatically

    Additional Backup¶

    For extra safety:

    • Export to CSV regularly
    • Save export file
    • Store in safe location

    Best Practices¶

    Keep Sync Enabled¶

    • Always leave sync on
    • Protects against data loss
    • Enables multi-device use

    Good Internet Connection¶

    • Sync works best with WiFi
    • Cellular works too
    • Avoid airplane mode

    Regular Checks¶

    • Verify sync occasionally
    • Check all devices have data
    • Ensure no errors

    Update Apps¶

    • Keep Money In & Out updated
    • Updates improve sync
    • Fix any sync issues

    FAQ¶

    Does sync cost money?¶

    No, iCloud sync is free with your iCloud account. Money In & Out uses minimal storage.

    Can I use without sync?¶

    Yes, sync is optional. The app works fine on a single device without iCloud.

    What if I change Apple ID?¶

    Data is tied to Apple ID. Changing ID means starting fresh unless you export/import data.

    Can I sync with Android?¶

    No, iCloud sync only works with Apple devices. Money In & Out is Apple-only.

    Is my data safe?¶

    Yes, data is encrypted and stored in your private iCloud account. Very secure.
